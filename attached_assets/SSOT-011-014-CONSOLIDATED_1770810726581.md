# SSOT-11: SECTION TYPE DEFINITIONS

## PURPOSE
Defines each section type (A1-A7, B1-B8) within the curriculum structure, specifying content requirements, image needs, and learning objectives.

## CORE PRINCIPLE: Geometric Element Theory
Every section connects to the theory: `TIMELESS → CULTURAL EXPRESSION → TECHNOLOGICAL INNOVATION → MODERN APPLICATION`.
- **Day A (Metaphor Focus):** Element as Sacred/Cultural Meaning.
- **Day B (Function Focus):** Element as Mathematical/Scientific Operation.

## DAY A: METAPHOR & CULTURE

### A1: MYTH HOOK
**Duration:** 5-7 mins
**Purpose:** Embed geometric element in sacred narrative.
**Content:** Narrative retelling, deity intro, element reveal.
**Image Needs:**
- **STORYBOARD:** 3-5 scenes (Opening, Conflict, Resolution).
- **MUSEUM_SEARCH:** 1-2 authentic deity depictions (e.g., Cylinder Seal).

### A2: SEL CONNECTION
**Duration:** 3-5 mins
**Purpose:** Internalize metaphor through emotion.
**Content:** Metaphor statement, discussion prompt, SEL skill.
**Image Needs:**
- **MUSEUM_SEARCH:** Artifact showing element in emotional context.
- **DIAGRAM (Optional):** Metaphor-feeling connection icon.

### A3: SYMBOLIC CHAIN
**Duration:** 5-7 mins
**Purpose:** Trace element from form to societal impact.
**Chain:** `Element → Property → Metaphor → Deity → Domain`.
**Image Needs:**
- **DIAGRAM:** Visual flow of the chain.
- **MUSEUM_SEARCH:** Element in domain context (e.g., legal stele).

### A4: DEITY ICONOGRAPHY
**Duration:** 5-7 mins
**Purpose:** Visual literacy - recognizing deities.
**Content:** Identifying features, semiotic meaning, element location.
**Image Needs:**
- **MUSEUM_SEARCH:** 3-5 distinct artifacts (Seals, Reliefs).
- **OVERLAY:** Annotated image highlighting features.

### A5: MATERIAL CULTURE
**Duration:** 7-10 mins
**Purpose:** Survey element across 6 artifact categories:
1. Monumental (Temples)
2. Administrative (Seals)
3. Devotional (Statues)
4. Funerary (Tombs)
5. Personal (Jewelry)
6. Astronomical (Calendars)
**Image Needs:**
- **MUSEUM_SEARCH:** 1-2 examples per category.

### A6: SEMIOTICS
**Duration:** 5-7 mins
**Purpose:** Decode visual meaning (Sign/Signifier/Signified).
**Image Needs:**
- **OVERLAY:** 2-3 annotated analyses.
- **MUSEUM_SEARCH:** Comparative symbol usage.

### A7: RITUAL PRACTICES
**Duration:** 5-7 mins
**Purpose:** Element in sacred and daily life.
**Image Needs:**
- **MUSEUM_SEARCH:** Ritual scenes (Ceremonial & Daily).
- **STORYBOARD (Optional):** Reconstructed scene if artifacts missing.

# SSOT-12: DEITY DESIGN & SPECIFICATIONS

## OVERVIEW
Definitions for the visual representation of Mesopotamian deities across all media (Artifacts, Storyboards, Diagrams).
**Critical Rule:** All depictions must adhere to iconographic consistency.

## DEITY REGISTRY

### 1. SHAMASH (Sun God)
*Domain: Justice, Truth, Sun* | *Element: Circle*
- **Primary Symbol:** Sun Disk (Circle with 4/8 rays/wavy lines).
- **Iconography:** Saw-toothed knife, Rod & Ring, Horned crown, Tiered robe.
- **Colors:** Gold (#FFD700), Warm Yellow, Deep Blue.
- **Key Poses:** Rising between mountains (Dawn), Enthroned (Justice).
- **Availability Verdict:** ✅ **HIGH.** Excellent museum artifacts exist (e.g., Tablet of Shamash).

### 2. ISHTAR (Venus/War/Love)
*Domain: War, Love, Venus* | *Element: Triangle (8-pointed star)*
- **Primary Symbol:** 8-Pointed Star.
- **Iconography:** Lions (flanking/standing on), Weapons (mace/bow), Wings (optional), Horned crown.
- **Colors:** Lapis Blue (#1E4D8C), Red (#B22222), Gold.
- **Key Poses:** "Queen of Heaven" (Frontal, winged), Warrior (on lion).
- **Availability Verdict:** ⚠️ **PARTIAL.** Good identification images, but "Descent" narrative scenes need creation/illustration.

### 3. NINGISHZIDA (Underworld)
*Domain: Underworld, Healing, Trees* | *Element: Spiral*
- **Primary Symbol:** Intertwined Serpents (Caduceus-like).
- **Iconography:** Horned serpent-dragons (Mushhushshu), Staff w/ snakes, Horned crown (sometimes w/ snakes).
- **Colors:** Deep Green (#1B4D3E), Brown, Black.
- **Key Poses:** Guardian at gate, Leading Gudea.
- **Availability Verdict:** ⚠️ **SUPPLEMENT NEEDED.** Symbolism is clear in artifacts (Gudea vase), but full deity portraits are rarer.

### 4. NABU (Scribe God)
*Domain: Wisdom, Writing* | *Element: Grid (Tablet)*
- **Primary Symbol:** Stylus & Clay Tablet.
- **Iconography:** Wedge symbol, Dragon-Snake (Mushhushshu) at feet, Horned crown.
- **Colors:** Deep Blue (#1A237E), Terracotta, Cream.
- **Key Poses:** Scribe (writing), Teacher.
- **Availability Verdict:** ⚠️ **SUPPLEMENT NEEDED.** Good symbols/statues, but narrative scenes (teaching) likely need Illustration.

### 5. MARDUK (Chief God)
*Domain: Creation, Kingship* | *Element: Symmetry*
- **Primary Symbol:** Spade (Marru) - Triangular head.
- **Iconography:** Mushhushshu Dragon, Rod & Ring, Elaborate multi-tiered crown.
- **Colors:** Royal Blue (#002366), Gold, Purple.
- **Key Poses:** Fighting Tiamat, Enthroned King.
- **Availability Verdict:** ⚠️ **SUPPLEMENT NEEDED.** Symbols excellent, but "Creation/Tiamat" scenes are rare/damaged; Illustration needed for Enuma Elish.

### 6. SIN (Moon God)
*Domain: Time, Cycles* | *Element: Tessellation (Phases)*
- **Primary Symbol:** Crescent Moon (Horizontal "Boat").
- **Iconography:** Bull, Lapis Lazuli Beard, Tripod/Lamp.
- **Colors:** Silver (#C0C0C0), Deep Blue (#191970).
- **Key Poses:** Sailing in crescent boat, Enthroned.
- **Availability Verdict:** ⚠️ **PARTIAL.** Symbols ubiquitous, figures available. Moon cycle requires **Diagrams**.

### 7. EA/ENKI (Water/Wisdom)
*Domain: Sweet Waters (Abzu), Craft* | *Element: Proportion*
- **Primary Symbol:** Flowing streams from shoulders/vase (w/ fish).
- **Iconography:** Goat-Fish (Capricorn), Turtle, Horned crown.
- **Colors:** Turquoise (#40E0D0), Blue, Green.
- **Key Poses:** Enthroned in Abzu, Creation of Man.
- **Availability Verdict:** ✅ **HIGH.** Excellent cylinder seals showing flowing water streams.

### 8. INANNA (Transformation Aspect)
*Domain: Descent, Rebirth* | *Element: Transformation*
- **Primary Symbol:** Gates/Hook/Vestments.
- **Iconography:** Removal of Regalia (7 items), Color change (Bright -> Dark -> Bright).
- **Availability Verdict:** ❌ **ILLUSTRATION REQUIRED.** Visuals for the specific "Descent/Stripping" sequence are virtually non-existent in suitable artifacts.

# SSOT-13: IMAGE & SCENE TAXONOMIES

## PART 1: IMAGE TYPE TAXONOMY (Technical/Source)
*Classification based on how the image is PRODUCED or ACQUIRED.*

### 1. MUSEUM_ARTIFACT
- **Source:** Searched (Museum APIs).
- **Specs:** Authentic photo, no modern reconstruction, >800px.
- **Use:** Core evidence, material culture.

### 2. DEITY_PORTRAIT
- **Source:** Searched + Verified.
- **Specs:** Clear iconographic features, indentifiable without label.
- **Use:** Deity introduction.

### 3. STORYBOARD_SCENE
- **Source:** Created (AI / Artist).
- **Specs:** Narrative moment, specific action, consistent style (Sketch/Illustration).
- **Use:** Myth retelling.

### 4. MATH_DIAGRAM
- **Source:** Created (Generated/Vector).
- **Specs:** Clean lines, labeled parts, grade-appropriate.
- **Subtypes:** Parts labeled, Proofs, Formulas.

### 5. TRANSFORMATION_SEQUENCE
- **Source:** Created (Animation/Series).
- **Specs:** Before -> Action -> After states.
- **Use:** Geometric operations.

### 6. PHYSICS_DIAGRAM
- **Source:** Created.
- **Specs:** Forces, motion paths, structural cross-sections.
- **Use:** STEM explanation.

### 7. INVENTION_PHOTO
- **Source:** Searched (Stock/Museum).
- **Specs:** Real-world object, clear element visibility.
- **Use:** Engineering connection.

### 8. OVERLAY_ANNOTATION
- **Source:** Composite (Artifact + Layer).
- **Specs:** Highlights on top of artifact photo (e.g., tracing a circle on a seal).
- **Use:** Analysis.

## PART 2: SCENE TYPE TAXONOMY (Content/Composition)
*Classification based on what is DEPICTED.*

### A. TABLEAU
- **Definition:** Static, iconic presentation. Central figure, symmetrical.
- **Examples:** Statue, Enthroned King.

### B. NARRATIVE
- **Definition:** Dynamic action, specific story moment.
- **Examples:** "Shamash Rising", "Slaying Tiamat".

### C. INTERACTION
- **Definition:** Relationship/Transaction. Figures engaging (worship, offering).
- **Examples:** Presentation scene, Divine Council.

### D. SYMBOL
- **Definition:** Abstract representation without figures.
- **Examples:** Solar disk, Crescent, Star.

### E. ARTIFACT_FOCUS
- **Definition:** The object itself is the subject.
- **Examples:** Pottery vessel, Tool.

### F. PROCEDURE_DEMO
- **Definition:** How-to steps.
- **Examples:** Hands using compass, Assembly instructions.

### G. COMPARISON
- **Definition:** Side-by-side analysis.
- **Examples:** Ancient vs Modern, Form A vs Form B.

# SSOT-14: CULTURAL AESTHETIC VOCABULARY

## PURPOSE
Defines the "Mesopotamian Look" for tagging and consistency.

## COMPOSITIONAL PATTERNS
- **Register Composition:** Stacked horizontal narrative bands.
- **Heraldic/Antithetical:** Symmetrical figures flanking a center (e.g., Lions around tree).
- **Presentation Scene:** Hierarchical sizes, figure leading another to a seated superior.
- **Processional:** Rhythm of figures moving in unison.

## FIGURAL CONVENTIONS
- **Profile Head / Frontal Eye:** Standard perspective.
- **Frontal Torso:** Shoulders often square to viewer.
- **Hierarchical Scale:** Size indicates importance (God > King > Human).
- **Musculature:** Stylized, emphasized (especially Assyrian).

## DECORATIVE & SYMBOLIC ELEMENTS
- **Borders:** Guilloche (braided), Rosettes.
- **Fills:** Scale pattern (Mountains/Water), Cross-hatching.
- **Nature:** Sacred Tree (stylized), Wavy lines (water).
- **Creatures:** Mushhushshu, Bull-man, Scorpion-man, Lion.

## COLOR PALETTE (Historical)
- **Lapis Blue (#1E4D8C):** Divine, Heavens.
- **Gold (#FFD700):** Solar, Divine radiance.
- **Turquoise (#40E0D0):** Water, Life.
- **Terracotta (#CC5500):** Earth, Clay.
- **Black/White:** Contrast, Eyes, Outlines.
